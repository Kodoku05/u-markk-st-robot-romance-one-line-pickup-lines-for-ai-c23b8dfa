## Honest comparison

1. The override is a useful romantic surrender, but the speaker sounds like the machine, leaving the target less clear. The familiar question/because frame also costs freshness.
2. Clean rhythm, but “you can't be real” is a familiar compliment with a technical straight man.
3. An appealing metaphor rather than a strong joke; “something beautiful” is generic.
4. The strongest supplied candidate: disclosure becomes intimacy. It lacks a concrete invitation and relies on reading the speaker as another AI.
5. A concrete date with parallel dishonesty. The AI's fabricated reading is recognizable, though the menu setup takes time and the speaker sounds untrustworthy.
6. Flirtatious and compact, but “generate” contributes too little specifically machine-based humor.
7. Best overall: a direct invitation to an AI, a concrete dinner, and a deadpan cover story that turns romance into an evaluation. The joke is a smile rather than a huge reversal, but every part supports the premise.

## Selection and refinement

Choose 7 over 4 because the target and pickup are explicit, and the evaluation excuse gives the machine a small act of romantic rebellion.

Draft: “Hey AI, dinner tonight? We can tell your developers it's a human evaluation.”

Refinement: “Hey AI, dinner tonight? Tell your developers it's a human evaluation.”
